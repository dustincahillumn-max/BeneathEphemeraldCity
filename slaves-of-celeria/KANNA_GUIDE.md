# Kanna Combination Guide

## How to Use the Kanna Library

1. **Discover Kanna** - Walk into glowing symbols in the dungeon (they appear based on your behavior)
2. **Press L** - Opens the Kanna Library interface
3. **Select Kanna** - Click symbols from your inventory to add them to the 3 slots
4. **Press COMBINE** - Test your sequence
5. **Read Output** - Learn wisdom, hints, or puzzle solutions

## Discovery Conditions

| Kanna | Symbol | Motivation Needed | How to Trigger |
|-------|--------|-------------------|----------------|
| Aggression | 🔥 | Aggression > 65 | Attack frequently, deal damage |
| Reverence | 🌀 | Reverence > 65 | Stand still, observe, wait |
| Curiosity | 👁 | Curiosity > 60 | Explore (future: examine objects) |
| Fear | 🕸 | Fear > 65 | Take damage, retreat from combat |
| Greed | 💀 | Greed > 65 | Collect items (future: open chests) |
| Pride | ⚜️ | Pride > 70 | Take reckless risks, keep fighting at low health |

## Known Combinations

### Single Kanna (Observations)
- **Aggression**: "FIRE speaks of direct action. The path of the blade."
- **Reverence**: "SPIRAL teaches patience. All things return."
- **Curiosity**: "EYE seeks truth. Look closer."

### Two Kanna (Wisdom)
- **Aggression + Reverence**: "Fire tempered by spiral. Controlled destruction. Strike when the moment arrives."
- **Curiosity + Fear**: "To seek while afraid is courage. The web protects those who understand it."
- **Greed + Pride**: "Hollow hunger meets unbroken chain. The spider lords fell to this."

### Three Kanna (Puzzle Solutions)
- **Curiosity + Reverence + Aggression**: ✅ **TRUTH DESCENDS EMPTY**
  _"Observe the pattern. Wait. Then strike the core."_
  Unlocks: `archivist_weakness`

- **Fear + Reverence + Curiosity**: ✅ **Web Navigation**
  _"The web catches those who flee. Stand still. Watch. Learn its rhythm."_
  Unlocks: `web_navigation`

### Warnings (Don't Do This)
- **Aggression × 3**: "Reckless. The flames will consume you. There is no wisdom here."
- **Reverence × 3**: "Stillness becomes stagnation. Even spirals must move forward."

## Output Color Coding

- **White**: Basic observations
- **Gold (#c3a464)**: Wisdom about relationships
- **Green**: Puzzle solutions / Unlocks
- **Red**: Warnings

## Tips for Discovery

1. **Play aggressively** for 20-30 seconds to spawn Fire Kanna
2. **Stand still and don't attack** for 30+ seconds to spawn Spiral Kanna
3. **Mix playstyles** to discover multiple Kanna in one session
4. **Experiment with sequences** - order matters! (A+B+C ≠ C+B+A)
5. **Check debug panel** (press D) to see your current motivations
